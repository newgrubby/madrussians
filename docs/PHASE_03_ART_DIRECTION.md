# Phase 03 — Art Direction Audit

Visual baseline: live production at `https://madrussians.vercel.app/`, captured at 1440×900, 1920×1080, and 390×844. Visual-language reference: `https://forest-joy.webflow.io/`. The reference informs scale, pacing, hierarchy, and scene construction only; its content and styling are not copied.

| Section | Current | Target | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | Strong photograph and identity, but the display title shares attention with route microcopy and navigation. | Editorial-cover hierarchy: photograph and two-line title dominate; secondary information recedes. | P1 |
| Facts | Correct structure, but numbers and labels read too small and utility-like. | Three quiet editorial columns with stronger numerals, precise rules, and more vertical air. | P1 |
| Expeditions | Five equal narrow cards expose title plus supporting information; hover expansion is restrained. | Photograph, number, and destination dominate by default; active card has a decisive width/exposure change and reveals metadata. | P0 |
| Story | Six media tiles compete and the middle state can read as scattered imagery instead of a poster. | Five purposeful layers around a larger, deliberately offset headline; clear foreground/background roles. | P0 |
| Format | The editorial index is sound, but the image stage does not feel dominant enough beside it. | Full-height image presence with a tighter index and clearer active/inactive contrast. | P1 |
| Archive | Distinct horizontal travel log, though captions and numbering are understated. | Preserve the log language while increasing destination/date/number authority. | P1 |
| About | The 61 treatment is strong but can become a more memorable graphic anchor. | Make 61 one of the page’s largest numeric forms while keeping the verified copy unchanged. | P1 |
| Reviews | Good pause and generous quote; controls still read slightly like a conventional slider. | More air and quieter editorial pagination. | P2 |
| Final CTA | Strong image finale; form competes slightly with the closing headline. | Preserve the form and honest demo state, but reduce its visual density and reinforce headline-first hierarchy. | P2 |
| Mobile | Functional composition, but Story still feels derived from desktop and some display breaks are cautious. | Independent mobile poster compositions, large expedition cards, and a concise visual finale without overflow. | P0 |
| Micro polish | Cyan and rules are consistent; some labels, borders, and hit areas vary in authority. | Consistent label scale, rule opacity, arrow treatment, and accessible interaction targets. | P2 |
